**Approval classification is a recommendation about governance**, not a claim that a person has signed off:

- **Adviser/researcher approval required:** changes to source eligibility, scientific claim scope, exposure handling, evidence semantics, approved search ranges, selection tolerances, or operational requirements. Record a dated joint decision or the project's documented delegated authority.
- **Implementation detail within the approved methodology:** exact file/schema mechanics and computational settings that implement an unchanged approved requirement. Researchers can document, review, and freeze these in a versioned protocol/configuration; no separate adviser amendment is inherently required. A setting that changes scientific meaning or selection policy escalates to the first category.
- **Mixed:** separate the methodological subdecision from mechanical implementation. Do not block a generic synthetic interface merely because its future research values remain pending; do not use that interface to apply unapproved research values.

To change a register status, record: chosen option and exact settings; decision maker/role; date; approval/reference location; rationale; protocol version; affected branches/runs; dependencies; and acceptance evidence. No approval reference for a new H1-H12 resolution was found in the reviewed documents. An eventual Rejected status also needs a documented rejection; omission is not rejection.

## Priority and dependency overview

| Gate | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Blocking** | Resolve the affected scientific/data issue before training on research data for that branch. Unaffected branches may proceed only when all of their own prerequisites are satisfied. |
| **Pre-implementation** | Freeze component semantics/settings before implementing that specific research behavior. Generic interfaces with synthetic placeholders remain possible. |
| **Pre-selection** | Freeze before candidate comparison. Because candidate outputs must not influence the rules, freeze these settings **before generating new comparative research-data fits or scores**, not after inspecting their rankings. |
| **Pre-final-evaluation** | Resolve before reserved calibration finalization, operational/system freeze, or confirmatory evaluation. Policies that govern data-derived values must be declared earlier. |

## H6. Practical equivalence and uncertainty

**Priority:** Pre-selection; freeze before new comparative research outputs. **Decision status:** Pending. **Approval evidence for proposed resolution:** None recorded.

1. **Exact unresolved issue.** The numerical `[delta_PR]`, paired resampling/weighting and interval method, equivalence comparison set, and the outcome when evidence is inconclusive rather than practically equivalent or materially different.
2. **Approved requirement.** P §4.3.5.2/Table 4.9 require pooled grouped-development OOF PR-AUC, 2,000 paired group-preserving bootstrap resamples, 95% intervals, and a justified predeclared practical-equivalence rule. Those constants are already approved and are not choices reopened here.

4. **Defensible options.** (A) Paired whole-group bootstrap with all sampled member artifacts and original group multiplicity, preserving the artifact-level estimand. (B) Equal-group-weight estimation only if explicitly approved as a different estimand. Use justified absolute AP tolerances in either case; do not equate a CI crossing zero with proof of equivalence.
5. **Recommendation and justification.** Recommend A, pooled non-interpolated AP as proposed under H9, fixed resampling seed 42, and percentile 95% intervals from 2,000 draws as a proposed implementation convention. A conservative proposed rule establishes equivalence only when the difference interval lies wholly inside `[-delta_PR,+delta_PR]`, and material superiority when wholly beyond the applicable boundary. Require an explicit predeclared handling of inconclusive comparisons before using this rule to lock a winner; do not add unplanned trials or select by preference. For one-class bootstrap draws, record them as invalid and use a predeclared validity rule; do not silently redraw until a favorable interval appears.
6. **Approval classification.** Adviser/researcher approval for tolerance, estimand, equivalence/multiplicity/inconclusive policy.
