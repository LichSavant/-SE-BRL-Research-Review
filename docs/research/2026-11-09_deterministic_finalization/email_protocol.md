## Prospective proposal, no implementation/adoption here

1. Accept source release/snapshot/access/license/schema/native-label facts and
   population/eligibility before touching development payloads.
2. Retain immutable source snapshots and parser/options/version receipts.
   Preserve a trustworthy native source-record key; otherwise capture a verified
   ingestion record locator before any filter, tied to snapshot/parser. This
   prospective locator is not evidence for an old post-filter rank match.
3. Canonical identity recommendation: full SHA-256 of versioned canonical JSON
   containing source namespace, snapshot identity, locator kind/version and
   verified original token/locator. Exclude target, group, partition, fold and
   content from identity. Stop on ambiguous/colliding identity evidence.
4. Preserve raw-to-clean-to-screened-to-feature linkage and reason-coded
   exclusions. Do not renumber retained rows. Keep normalization hashes as
   separate duplicate-family evidence; they are not unique original identities.
5. Freeze normalization, exact duplicate conflict handling, documented near-
   duplicate algorithm/configuration and admissible campaign/template/domain/
   source relationships. Reconcile accepted edges into conservative components
   before any allocation. Do not infer new relationships or numeric thresholds
   from current test performance. Record source-class composition and residual
   limits where source fields do not support complete grouping.
6. Obtain acceptance for group-constrained, source-class-aware allocation under
   the proposal's development/calibration/test roles. Target proportions are
   protocol goals, not permission to split a component. Five development folds
   must preserve groups and have feasible classes. This sprint does **not**
   generate groups, assignments, folds or claim such feasibility on real data.
7. Accept historical exposure disposition first. A new split/ID/seed cannot
   reseal already evaluated records. Recommend exploratory/internal claims
   unless authentic evidence and adviser acceptance support a genuinely
   unexposed evaluation scope. No new/unexposed dataset is presumed available.
8. Custodian delivers only the authorized development scope; Dhanwil verifies
   metadata/hash/eligibility/feature alignment without fitting. Reserved
   calibration/test payloads stay outside the model-selection loader. Final
   calibration/threshold/evaluation require separate authorization and freeze.
